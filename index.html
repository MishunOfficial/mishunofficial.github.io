I STAND BY MY WORK

‪The Mishun project is made by me in its entirety. I write and produce the music that keeps playing in my head, I write the scripts, draw the artworks and create the animated videos. It’s a long, sometimes tedious process of doing something, scrapping it, starting again, sometimes even looking at the finished thing and throwing it all in the bin cause something is off, or it doesn’t fit…
But I love it, and I would never let AI (Abominable Intelligence) take the fun away from me. I refuse to sell my soul and choose a shortcut, I reject the convenience in the name of creativity, and it will always stay that way.
